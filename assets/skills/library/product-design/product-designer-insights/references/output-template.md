# Output template — Full synthesis

Use this structure unless the user asks for a different format or another output mode fits better.

# Product Designer Insights

## 1. Executive summary
The product problem, the strongest evidence, and the most important design implication — in a few sentences.

## 2. Source register
| ID | Source | Type/date | What it supports | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Product context
- Product/feature:
- Users/roles:
- Desired outcome:
- Workflow:
- Platform:
- Constraints:
- Missing context:

## 4. Requirements and constraints
Separate sourced requirements, inferred requirements (`Inferred — validate`), non-functional requirements, dependencies and open decisions.

## 5. Evidence-backed findings
| ID | Finding | Evidence/source | Confidence | Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. User problem statements
`[User/role] needs a way to [goal] because [evidenced barrier], which leads to [documented consequence].` Include source references.

## 7. Journey / workflow implications
A table (stage, action, system response, friction, source, opportunity) if the evidence supports one; otherwise explain the journey information gap.

## 8. Design opportunities
| Opportunity | Linked finding | Desired outcome | Exploration options | Validation question |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 9. Recommendations and next steps
Group by discipline. Separate immediate actions from items that need validation.

## 10. Open questions and assumptions
Unresolved questions, assumptions, contradictions and missing evidence.

## 11. Evidence limitations
What cannot responsibly be concluded from the supplied material.

---

# Design-ready brief (when asked to hand off to design)

1. Problem and target outcome
2. Users and context supported by evidence
3. Prioritised requirements (only if defensible)
4. Key workflows and states to explore
5. Constraints and dependencies
6. Content and data needs
7. Accessibility and localisation considerations
8. Success measures and instrumentation gaps
9. Assumptions and validation plan
